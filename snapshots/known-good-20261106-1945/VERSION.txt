Changes since 20261106-1905:

  * battle shouts are black ink again. A text control's +0x14 colour word is
    the one X_TEXTCTRL_DrawTextControl (0x1447a8) passes to GRPX_SetFontColor,
    packed (a<<24)|(b<<16)|(g<<8)|r. X_TEXTCTRL_InitTextControl writes
    0xFF000000 (opaque BLACK) as the default and X_TEXTCTRL_GetColorFromCode's
    table at 0xbb9c8 confirms the byte order ($R = 0xFF0000FF, $B =
    0xFF000000). text_draw_engine() takes 0xRRGGBBAA, so black was read as
    R=255,G=0,B=0 -- red text on the white balloon. tctrl_colour() now
    byte-swaps the word for nx_textctrl_draw() and desc_draw_block(). The
    textctrl path does NOT apply text_colour()'s black->white lift.

  * the plain tips' 60% black plate is 34 px tall. It is NOT a different
    rectangle from the tutorial hint: both are quads submitted by
    SGL_grpFillRect (0x12b484 -> glDrawArrays at 0x12b63c), black at 60%
    (0x99000000), 20 px high. The tutorial one is 977 px wide, the plain tip is
    FULL SCREEN (1137 px), and bar_expand()'s width cap was 1080 -- so the plain
    tip was silently skipped. The cap is now 1280 (the engine's screen width).
    UI_DrawTextBackground (0x143b30) independently confirms 34 px is the
    engine's own text-background height (w3 = 0x22).
    A rectangle-level override of SGL_grpFillRect was tried first and REMOVED:
    it also caught the quest page's three 476x15/18/15 title strips in brown
    (0xe5315069) and grey (0x7f6f6f6f), stacking a grey plate over the text.

  * the quest page's title is back in its MAIN bar. text_draw_engine_ex()'s
    wrap_extra is a frame-global push-down that is only cleared when the next y
    goes BACKWARDS. The quest description wraps to 5 lines and left 4 lines
    (~100 px) behind; the next control (the title / list row) is drawn at a
    LARGER y, so it inherited the whole offset. nx_textctrl_draw() now clears
    the accumulator after every control (text_set_wrap_extra(0) then (1)).
    Log proof, same frame:
      quest: [grpx]    at 193,173 col ffffffff '刺杀格里普顿伯爵'
      quest: [textctrl] at 682,168/193/218/243 ... (the five description lines)

  * tools/win_place.py decodes tasklist with the system ANSI code page (mbcs)
    instead of UTF-8, which crashed on zh-CN Windows; the second-screen test
    window can always be placed again.

Known open item (engine-side art, not port code):

  * a SHORT battle shout ("杀了他") shows a white plate behind the bubble, its
    four corners sticking out. The balloon background is a nine-slice with 35 px
    corners drawn into a 160x45 box: the corners overlap by 25 px and the middle
    tiles that would cover their square edges are only 10..20 px wide. Wide
