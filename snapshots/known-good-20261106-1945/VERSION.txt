Changes since 20261106-1905:

  * battle shouts are black ink again. A text control's +0x14 colour word is
    the one X_TEXTCTRL_DrawTextControl (0x1447a8) passes to GRPX_SetFontColor,
    packed (a<<24)|(b<<16)|(g<<8)|r. X_TEXTCTRL_InitTextControl writes
    0xFF000000 (opaque BLACK) as the default and X_TEXTCTRL_GetColorFromCode's
    table at 0xbb9c8 confirms the byte order ($R = 0xFF0000FF, $B =
    0xFF000000). text_draw_engine() takes 0xRRGGBBAA, so black was read as
    R=255,G=0,B=0 -- red text on the white balloon. tctrl_colour() now
    byte-swaps the word for nx_textctrl_draw() and desc_draw_block(). The
    textctrl path does NOT apply text_colour()'s black->white lift.

  * the plain tips' 60% black plate is now 34 px tall. SGL_grpFillRect
    (0x12b484) is the single exit for every solid rectangle (GRPX_FillRect /
    FillRectAlpha / FillRectRGBAlpha tail-call it); the runtime return address
    in "bar: quad 80,152-1057,172 ... ra 0x4ceb35f640" is SGL_grpFillRect+0x1bc.
    UI_DrawTextBackground (0x143b30) asks GRPX_FillRectRGBAlpha for h = 0x22,
    so 34 px is the engine's own text background, while the hint plates were
    submitted at 20. bar_expand() only matched quads with a black
    GL_CURRENT_COLOR, which the tinted plain tips never have. The override
    nx_sgl_fillrect() grows short wide translucent plates around their centre
    to cfg.bar_h at the rectangle's source, before the real function builds
    its four vertices. Bounded "fillrect:" log records parameters + caller
    vaddr.

  * REGRESSION FIX in this rebuild: the first attempt accepted any short, wide,
    translucent rectangle and broke the quest page -- its title bar is three
    476x15 / 476x18 / 476x15 strips at y 164/179/197 in brown (0xe5315069) and
    grey (0x7f6f6f6f); growing and shifting them stacked a grey plate over the
    quest text. The condition is now exactly the hint plate: h == 20, w >= 200,
    and a pure-black colour with a non-trivial alpha (0xAA000000, e.g.
    0x99000000). The quest strips share none of those.
